LONGER BRAND BACKGROUND
Cielle Candles is a fragrance-focused home brand founded by Connie Kersee. Rooted in the belief that a beautiful scent can change how a space feels, the brand offers carefully crafted candles, wax melts, and reed diffusers for everyday rituals, thoughtful gifting, and inviting spaces. Its soy-wax candles are hand-poured in small batches, with scent profiles designed for many moods, from soft florals to warm woods. Cielle Candles invites customers to slow down, set the mood, and enjoy a little everyday luxury.

FOUNDER BIO
Connie Kersee is the Founder & Artisan behind Cielle Candles. She leads the brand with an appreciation for scent, craftsmanship, and beautiful presentation. Her approach centers on creating home fragrances that help make ordinary moments feel personal and memorable.
