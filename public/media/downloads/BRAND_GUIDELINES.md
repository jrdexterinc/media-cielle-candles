# Cielle Candles | Basic media usage guidelines

Updated: October 2026

## Brand identity

- Brand: **Cielle Candles**
- Founder: **Connie Kersee, Founder & Artisan**
- Brand line: **Hand-poured with intention. Made to set the mood.**
- Colors featured in approved logo: warm rust, deep umber, ivory and black.
- Product categories: Candles, Wax Melts, Reed Diffusers.

## Asset use

- Use the supplied official logo artwork. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects to it.
- Allow generous clear space around the logo. Use the dark-on-light mark on pale backgrounds and light-on-dark mark on dark surfaces.
- The three founder portraits were created through AI-assisted wardrobe and background edits from reference photographs supplied by the business. Do not present them as unaltered documentary photography.
- Editorial and event-promotional uses related to Cielle Candles are welcome; obtain permission for unrelated commercial placements or endorsements.
- Product claims, ingredients, price, and availability should be verified against current brand listings.
